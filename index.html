The four questions

1 · Customer JourneyA gig delivery driver still has orders to deliver when a parade or live event closes part of the city. We fit at two moments: deciding whether to accept an order, and choosing how to drive each leg.
2 · DistributionPlatforms and drivers pick us whenever an event disrupts the city: we know which streets close at what time and where drivers can still cross, which standard routing can't see. The platform adds us to its driver tools, so we don't win drivers one by one.
3 · ValuePlatform: fewer late orders, refunds, cancellations and lost subscribers. Driver: higher real $/hr, fewer bad orders. Restaurant & customer: food arrives on time and hot. City: fewer cars pushing into closed streets. Next: drivers report closures to keep the map current.
4 · ControlLean into: the platform's existing model. We suggest routes, stop order and which orders to take, without changing how orders are assigned. Safeguard: never route into a closure, the driver approves every change, customer messages need a tap, pickups stay before drop-offs.
